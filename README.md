# WiCSE Post Fair Follow Up Guide

An interactive guide from **WiCSE (Women in Computer Science and Engineering) at the University of Florida** to help members turn career fair conversations into real opportunities.

You talked to recruiters. Now what? This guide walks you through exactly what to do after the fair: when to follow up, what to say, how to connect on LinkedIn, and how to keep track of everyone you met.

**Live guide:** [dalilaportal.github.io/wicse-follow-up-guide](https://dalilaportal.github.io/wicse-follow-up-guide)

---

## What's Inside

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Booth Checklist** | Checkboxes for what to capture at each booth. Your progress saves automatically. |
| **Email Builder** | Fill in a few fields and get a polished follow up email with a live preview and one click copy. |
| **LinkedIn Note Builder** | Write a connection request with a built in 300 character counter so it never gets cut off. |
| **Follow Up Date Calculator** | Enter the fair date and get your full follow up timeline mapped out. |
| **Common Mistakes** | The things that quietly cost people opportunities, and how to avoid them. |
| **What If FAQ** | Answers for the awkward moments: no reply, forgot a name, lost a business card, and more. |
| **Recruiter Tracker** | An editable table to log every contact, with status tracking, add and delete rows, and a safe clear all button. |

---

## How to Use It

1. Open the live link above on your laptop or phone.
2. Work through the sections in order, or jump to what you need.
3. Your checklist and tracker save in your own browser, so you can close the page and pick up where you left off.

> **Privacy note:** Everything you type stays on your device. Nothing is sent to a server or shared with WiCSE. That also means your saved data won't follow you to a different browser or device, and clearing your browser data will reset it.

## For Maintainers

This is a single, self contained `index.html` file (HTML, CSS, and JavaScript). No build tools, no dependencies, no setup.

**To update the guide:**
1. Edit `index.html` locally in any text editor.
2. In this repo, click **Add file → Upload files** and upload the new version with the same name (`index.html`).
3. Click **Commit changes**. The live site updates within a minute or two.

**Hosting:** Served through GitHub Pages from the `main` branch, root folder (**Settings → Pages**).
